Featured Project: RadioAtlas

A SwiftUI app that turns map exploration, live radio, and personal media into one listening experience.

iOS App

A map-first listening experience

In Active Development

RadioAtlas is a SwiftUI iOS app that lets users explore 9 curated places and 54 radio stations across 28 countries through a full-screen, map-first interface. It combines live radio streams for stations with bundled offline audio for places, so users can move between geographic exploration, ambient listening, and music playback in one polished experience.

Project snapshot

54 stations 28 countries 9 curated places Live + offline audio Background playback Persistent personalization

Map-First Exploration

Users can browse content in lists or jump straight into a full-screen map with tappable pins for places and stations.

Hybrid Audio Playback

The app supports both live radio streams and bundled offline tracks, with background playback and a persistent mini player.

Personalized Pins

Users can save favorites, revisit recents, attach photos or videos to pins, and let the first attachment become the pin thumbnail.

Persistent Customization

Map style, custom pins, category changes, and per-pin sound selections are saved across launches for a more personal experience.

Core stack

Swift SwiftUI MapKit AVFoundation AVKit PhotosUI Alamofire UserDefaults Persistence

Why this project matters

RadioAtlas reflects how I approach product building end to end: I designed a map-first workflow, implemented streaming and local audio playback, built persistence for favorites, recents, and media, and focused on polish through onboarding, settings, accessibility, and resilient playback behavior.
